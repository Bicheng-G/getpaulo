4Moment — one-tap swap applied

Tapping another temptation sticker swaps both cards in place — art and star value update (ride-on 2★ → snack 1★). Optional, never blocks; the one-choice-per-moment write guard is untouched. This is why there is no separate picker screen: the default covers most moments, the strip covers the rest.

15Fulfillment — spending the promise

Funded ≠ fulfilled: only the real-world purchase confirmed by the child completes the journey (unchanged). New: the spend is explicit — the wish’s target leaves the balance, and what remains stays with the child.
