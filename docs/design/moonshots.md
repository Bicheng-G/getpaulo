# Psychological Moonshot Playbook

**Status:** LIVE · method + pattern reference.
**Read this when:** designing a new feature or fixing a moment that feels uncertain, invisible, anxious, uncared-for, or unfinished — before proposing a large feature or expensive engineering change.
**Update this when:** a new pattern ships or a pattern's integrity boundary changes.
**Governing rule ([principles.md#moonshots](principles.md)):** improve how the truth is experienced; never improve the feeling by distorting the truth.

A **psychological moonshot** is a small, truthful, low-cost intervention that creates disproportionate improvement in perceived value, reassurance, satisfaction, or care by addressing the user's emotional and cognitive experience rather than changing the underlying physical process.

Required framing: felt experience is part of product reality · remove uncertainty before adding speed · make care and causality visible · prefer low-cost high-leverage interventions · preserve agency and semantic integrity · never use perception to conceal broken reality.

## High-leverage psychological states {#states}

Prioritise moments where the family experiences: **uncertainty** ("Did it work? What happens next?") · **invisible progress** · **loss anxiety** ("Will the stars or memory disappear?") · **lack of agency** ("Did Paulo decide for me?") · **lack of care** ("Processed, not acknowledged") · **cognitive overload** ("What do I say now?") · **lack of closure** ("It ended but didn't feel complete"). These are usually better solved by a precise status, representation, transition, phrase, or ritual than by a new destination or content library.

## Paulo patterns {#patterns}

| Moment | Small intervention | Psychological effect | Integrity boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capture processing | Truthful stages ("Finding the object", "Making the sticker") + continuous subject handoff | Visible care and causality instead of black-box waiting | No fake delay or invented step |
| Automatic naming | "Paulo's best guess — change it if it's wrong," alternatives, typed-input protection | AI error becomes collaborative suggestion, not loss of control | Never overwrite family input or claim certainty |
| Moment of Choice | Matched consequence previews before selection | Removes mental arithmetic while preserving equal legitimacy | No glow, amber, or motion advantage for Save |
| Choice-moment interlude | Rotate handoff + one guided breath revealing the wish ([interlude.md](interlude.md)) | Converts a rushed flash decision into a calm embodied ritual | Pause disguised as care, not friction; no Save priming |
| Wish progress | A walk/count carries progress while the real sticker stays fully coloured; gradual colour return remains a default-off comparison | Keeps the wish recognisable while testing whether photo transformation adds understanding | Any variant represents the actual pooled ledger and must not make Save more persuasive |
| Save confirmation | "Two stars joined your promise" + one causal motion | Consequence legible without praising character | No routine confetti or moral verdict |
| Play Now confirmation | Equally warm, complete confirmation | Silence never reads as punishment or disappointment | No implied lost status or corrective prompt |
| Wish rest/release | "Your stars stay yours" at the decision point | Removes loss anxiety; supports reprioritisation | Must be literally true in the ledger |
| Ledger and sync | Clear recorded/synced state, honest recovery message | Effort and promises never feel vanished | Never show success before durable acceptance |
| Parent micro-prompt | One sentence at the exact hot moment | Reduces parental load and uncertainty | Must shorten interaction, not start a lecture |
| Ready / fulfilment | First-time-only ceremony and keepsake transition | Rare milestone gets emotional weight and closure | No repeated amplification or Save moralisation |
| Little Wins revisit | Concrete source evidence beside a short reflection | Ordinary mastery becomes weighty and retrievable | No invented strength or unsupported identity narrative |
| Family Rules agreement | Visible shared-promise confirmation | Agreement feels mutual, deliberate, trustworthy | Rules stay explainable; change visibly |

## Moonshot test {#test}

An intervention qualifies only when **all** hold:

1. Names a specific psychological friction (not vague "delight").
2. Materially smaller and cheaper than changing the underlying physical process.
3. Improves comprehension, reassurance, agency, perceived care, or closure.
4. Adds little or no user maintenance.
5. Truthful, reversible where appropriate, understandable.
6. Does not privilege Save, increase child dependence, or create compulsive engagement.
7. Objective failures remain visible and actionable.
8. Measurable through the intended psychological outcome, not only taps or conversion.

## Measurement {#measurement}

Match the metric to the intended state: uncertainty → "I knew what was happening" · trust → "I believed the star/photo/promise was safely recorded" · agency → "My child felt the choice was theirs" · perceived care → "Paulo felt thoughtful without more work" · felt progress → "My child could see how the action changed the wish" · closure → "The moment felt complete and we returned offline." Where practical, compare against the same process without the intervention. If it improves feeling while worsening understanding, agency, honesty, or parent burden, it is not a successful moonshot.

## Prioritisation rule {#prioritisation}

Before building a large feature, ask: can one truthful sentence, status, preview, transition, representation, or ritual remove most of the psychological friction? Fix the physical system when it is objectively broken; use a moonshot when the system works but its value, causality, reliability, or care is not yet felt.
